ANTONIO GARCÍA FERNÁNDEZ
DOB: 29.03.2003 // A CORUÑA

I am an industrial design engineer with a degree from the Escuela Universitaria de Diseño Industrial at the Universidad de la Coruña. I am passionate about innovation and solving real problems, creating functional products with impactful aesthetics.

I am creative, consistent, and cooperative. I enjoy finding innovative solutions but also thrive in team environments, exchanging ideas and information. I am a visionary who knows how to unlock the potential in both people and projects.

I love to start my own projects to strengthen my personal brand and gain experience. From a futuristic amphibious motorcycle designed for SEAT/CUPRA to 3D printing strategies for Navantia's autonomous vessels — I pursue ideas that push boundaries.
