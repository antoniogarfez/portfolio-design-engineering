Exercise Overview

A relief design exercise exploring three-dimensional surface composition, layering, and texture. Two initial proposals were developed before selecting the final direction.

Proposal 1: Ghosting

A flowing, organic relief inspired by the psychological phenomenon of being "ghosted" — communication silence rendered as abstract flowing forms that dissolve and reappear across the panel surface.

Proposal 2: Spammer

A chaotic, dense texture inspired by spam noise and data overflow — layered wave-like forms that compress and expand, referencing the visual noise of over-communication.

Final Development

The selected concept was refined into a complex architectural relief with organic, flowing channel forms — referencing contemporary biomorphic architecture (Zaha Hadid-influenced). Modelled in SolidWorks and realised as a physical maquette through CNC milling of foam, sanded and finished to a smooth architectural surface.
