Cupra Proraptor
Amphibious
Motorcycle

Contextualisation

The technological revolution has penetrated our lives faster than many can adapt. The sensations, experiences and entertainment offered by new technologies, social media and video games are causing people in search of adrenaline and new emotions to increase their demand for increasingly innovative and emotionally charged products.

This project focuses on designing a vehicle capable of moving across multiple terrains and environments, giving the user a freedom never seen before, and society a product of real utility and value. Jet skis are among the most fun and agile aquatic vehicles available, but they are rarely used in daily life and rarely form a personal bond with the user the way motorcycles do.

The User

The target user is shaped by three key elements: the principles and norms of contemporary society; the ideals and habits of their social circle, which belongs to the Generation Z environment; and their individual traits, experiences, and personal preferences.

This profile is a young person with academic and professional ambitions who values independence greatly. Freedom is a fundamental principle. They play video games, have a keen interest in new technologies — especially AI and IoT — and are athletic, enjoy adrenaline, and travel for experiences. They ride motorcycles and connect with others who share that passion.

Sketch Development

The concept evolved through iterative refinement, balancing aesthetic ambition with functional requirements. Early decisions focused on establishing a distinctive visual language — aggressive yet futuristic, inspired by both marine creatures and aerospace engineering.

One of the key turning points was the redesign of the front fairing, which originally exposed the front wheel. The decision to enclose the front wheel gave the motorcycle a more cohesive, aerodynamic silhouette — reinforcing its futuristic identity. This change also improved the vehicle's hydrodynamic profile for water transitions.

Development of the Concept

A critical functional challenge was managing heat buildup around the front wheel area, particularly when transitioning between land and water modes. An integrated cooling system was developed, using ventilation channels embedded within the fairing to regulate temperature without compromising the sealed design required for water operation.

Other refinements included adjusting the rear tyre thickness — balancing durability on rough terrain with reduced weight for hydrofoil lift. The exhaust system was also repositioned to prevent water ingestion during amphibious transitions. Each decision was validated through digital simulations and physical prototyping, ensuring the final design is as functional as it is expressive.

Final Concept

The key aspect of the project, and what makes Proraptor unique, is its three integrated driving modes, which allow it to master and transition seamlessly from one environment to another. The aquatic systems are powered both pneumatically and by a hybrid electric motor.

01
Road Mode
The user rides on land normally. The hydrofoils and flotation system are fully retracted into the fairing for optimal aerodynamics and a clean visual profile.
02
Flotation Mode
Upon water entry, pneumatic lateral floats deploy automatically — inflated like bellows from nylon-urethane — providing initial stability. The rear wheel propels via its specially designed rim.
03
Hydrofoil Mode
Once speed is reached, retractable hydrofoils generate hydrodynamic lift, elevating the motorcycle above the water surface. The C-Pod dual contra-rotating propeller provides thrust and precision.

Technical Engineering

Hydrofoil System

Based on Candela C-8 proportions. Retractable T-shaped foils inspired by Rinspeed Splash hide within the fairing when not in use. Reference dimensions: 1300mm / 1950mm / 1650mm foil geometry.

C-Pod Propulsion

Dual contra-rotating propeller inspired by the Candela C-8's C-Pod motor. Real-time sensors adjust each propeller's speed independently for trajectory correction and stability at high speed.

Hybrid Drivetrain

Twin-cylinder inline combustion engine assisted by an electric motor. The electric motor also powers the C-Pod propeller in hydrofoil mode, creating a seamless hybrid system.

Smart Fairing

Front wheel fairing acts as a hydrodynamic "prow," protecting brake discs from seawater corrosion. Adaptive scales open automatically based on disc temperature and speed — similar to the Mercedes-Benz Vision AVTR system.

Proraptor — The Concept

Proraptor unites myth, technology and nature. The name references Prometheus (fire stealer, technology) and the Velociraptor (agility, freedom). The orange represents the fire of Olympus; the black represents elegance and power. The logo — a stylised torch — expresses energy and innovation.

The name Proraptor is a deliberate fusion of two powerful symbols: Prometheus, the Titan who stole fire from the gods and gave technology to humanity — representing innovation, courage and the spark of creation. And Velociraptor, an agile, intelligent predator — representing speed, freedom and instinct.

The colour palette reinforces this duality: orange echoes the flame of Olympus — energy, danger, passion. Black represents elegance, power and the unknown depths.

Proraptor App

A companion app offers real-time vehicle information, mode switching (land/water), navigation and marine route planning, simulation, and community connectivity — uniting vehicle and rider into a single ecosystem.
