Urban Waste
Bins
50, 60 & 120L

Project Overview

A family of urban waste bins in three capacities — 50L, 60L, and 120L — designed for public spaces, parks, and urban areas. Developed in collaboration with FrutoDS and Formato Verde.

Design Approach

The structure is elongated and narrow, meeting capacity requirements while minimising footprint in public thoroughfares. The design avoids occupying unnecessary space on sidewalks and public paths.

Fabricated through plastic injection moulding, the form language is clean and geometric, adaptable to different contexts: Cubo (cubic bins for urban plazas), Parque (park variants), and Urbanización (residential area variants).

Market Research

An exhaustive competitive analysis was conducted, benchmarking 8+ manufacturers including Benito, Glasdon, FARO, Vestre, and others — covering form, material, capacity, mounting systems, and price points across the European urban furniture market.

Technical Deliverables

Final deliverables included multi-view technical drawings with dimension callouts, 3D models in SolidWorks and Rhinoceros, rendered presentations, and competitive benchmarking report.
