Project Overview

Three new product lines for the E-Comfort electric thermal emitter range, designed for Electrodomésticos Viveiro — a Galician manufacturer of heating appliances. The challenge was to differentiate three distinct market tiers (creative, standard, and premium) with coherent yet distinctive design identities.

Design Concept

The creative line design plays with surface perception within the fairing panels. A single mould gives the impression of two overlapping elements — a sophisticated optical illusion that reduces manufacturing complexity while elevating visual interest.

The panel structure uses EV (Electric Vehicle-inspired) elements combined with plastic-moulded fairings, creating a product that reads as both high-technology and domestically refined.

Target User

Young adults with minimalist and modern aesthetics. Designed for large rooms, open-plan living areas, and dining rooms, the emitters are intended to complement contemporary interior design rather than disappear against the wall.

CMF

Colour variants include: white/grey (clean minimal), slate/dark grey (sophisticated), and accent colour variations (yellow, red) for statement pieces. The interchangeable fairing panel concept allows personalisation at retail level.
