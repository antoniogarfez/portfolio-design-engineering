Exercise Overview

A fundamental graphic design exercise exploring the Bauhaus principles of point, line and plane as compositional elements. Three separate proposals were developed, each focusing on one element while using the others as supporting geometry.

Proposal Punto: Influencer

Inspired by social influence and virality — circles of varying scale cluster and disperse across the composition, referencing the diffusion of influence through digital networks. Large black dots anchor the composition while micro-dots create density and visual noise.

Proposal Línea: Report

Inspired by breaking news and urgent communication — bold diagonal lines of varying weight create tension and urgency. Background fine-line grids (resembling ECG signals or seismic charts) contrast with heavy black crossing diagonals.

Proposal Plano: MMO

Inspired by massively multiplayer online game maps and data visualisation — triangular plane elements of varying sizes tile across the composition, creating density gradients that reference digital territory and game-map topology.
